ValhallowedIngress and boundaries

One way in, a contract at every crossing

Settings, secrets and operator actions enter VHD in exactly one place, the ingress, and are validated there. Inside, every crossing between two parts is a boundary with one typed contract, one validator on the receiving side and a stated failure behaviour.

Boundaries inside VHD

The boundary standard

  • One typed contract, versioned with the release, with a contract test in the pipeline.
  • The receiving side validates. Nothing trusts the sender.
  • One owner.
  • A stated failure behaviour. Observations fail soft: drop and count, and the game never waits. Actions fail closed: refuse and record.

What the boundaries never allow

  • A child reading the manifest or the secrets file. Children read their generation and the secrets scoped to it, nothing else.
  • Any service but the data layer changing the database schema.
  • Anything but the broker creating a command, or anything but the plugin executing one. Markers and journal entries stay records.
  • An outside service becoming required. Chat relay, assistant and sign-in each switch off cleanly.
  • A boundary without a contract test in the pipeline.
